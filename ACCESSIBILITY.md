Accessibility Audit

I used Google PageSpeed Insights to audit my portfolio website.

Website: https://toby-bourne-portfolio.netlify.app/

Results

My website received a score of 100/100 for accessibility.

The audit showed 19 passed checks, 10 manual checks, and 44 checks that were not applicable.

Some of the areas that passed included:
Images having alt text.
Sufficient color contrast.
Links having descriptive names.
Headings appearing in the correct order.
Proper HTML language and main landmark attributes.

Manual Testing

I also tested my website using only a keyboard. I used Tab and Shift + Tab to navigate between elements and Enter and Space to interact with links and controls.

Keyboard navigation worked as expected. Focus was visible, and the navigation order was logical.

The initial accessibility audit did not identify any issues that required fixing.
